# LandTech 2026 - National Land Registry & Verification System ("PorchaAI & DeedTrust")

## Project Overview
Built for the **LandTech 2026 Innovation Hackathon** (Daffodil International University)[cite: 6]. 
- **Track:** Digital Land Records, Verification & Digitization[cite: 6]
- **Core Problem Modules:**
  - **P1 (Digitize Old Records):** AI-powered OCR parsing of handwritten and printed colonial-era Bangla khatians, porchas, and deeds into searchable structured schemas.
  - **P2 & P3 (Verify Deeds & Catch Fraud):** Cryptographic SHA-256 deed hashing and automated chain-of-title lineage mapping to stop double-selling and fraudulent transfers[cite: 11].
  - **P5 (Check Before You Buy):** Citizen risk dashboard auditing court dispute records, Khas land statuses, and government acquisition warnings prior to property purchase[cite: 11].

---

## Key Features
1. **PorchaAI Document Forensics Studio:** Upload or select historical records to parse messy Bangla handscript into JSON attributes automatically.
2. **Automated Chain-of-Title Lineage Graph:** Traces ownership flow from CS to RS/BS khatians[cite: 5, 11].
3. **Interactive Cadastral Map & Risk Inspector:** Leaflet.js-powered spatial plot lookup[cite: 10].
4. **Government Portal Aesthetic:** Styled after official Bangladeshi government portals (`bangladesh.gov.bd`) using bottle green (`#006A4E`) and crimson red (`#F42A41`).

---

## Tech Stack
- **Frontend & AI Interface:** HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Vanilla JavaScript, Leaflet.js
- **Version Control:** Git & GitHub[cite: 12]
- **Data Compliance:** Strictly adheres to dummy data protocols (no real NIDs, phone numbers, or bulk government scraping)[cite: 12].
